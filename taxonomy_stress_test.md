# TakeMeter taxonomy stress test

These eight posts were generated by AI to probe the definitions in `README.md`.
They are synthetic and must not be put in `labels.csv` as real r/workout posts. For the deciding rule, the distinction between `help_request` and `discussion_prompt` is whether
the author wants help with their own unresolved issue, not merely whether they
mention a personal experience.

| # | Synthetic boundary post | Proposed label | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I switched to incline dumbbells and my chest finally grew. Has anyone else seen that change?" | `discussion_prompt` | The request is for others' experiences, despite the author's result. (does not ask for help) |
| 2 | "I switched to incline dumbbells and my chest finally grew. Flat bench was a waste of time for me." | `sharing` | It reports a result and view without requesting a response. |
| 3 | "I switched to incline dumbbells and my chest finally grew. Should I remove flat bench from my program?" | `help_request` | The requested answer guides the author's own program. (a personal question) |
| 4 | "I missed a week because I was sick. How do you all handle the guilt of missing sessions?" | `help_request` | The author has an unresolved feeling of guilt and seeks strategies or reassurance for it. (a personal question) |
| 5 | "I missed a week because I was sick. Should I make up the missed sessions or resume my schedule?" | `help_request` | It asks for a next step in the author's training. (a personal question) |
| 6 | "I missed a week because I was sick. The rest helped, and I came back stronger." | `sharing` | It reports an outcome without asking for advice or discussion. |
| 7 | "Here's my four-day split and my goal is stronger legs. What would you change?" | `help_request` | It directly asks for personal feedback on the author's existing plan. |
| 8 | "I gained strength on a four-day split. Is four days enough for most people?" | `discussion_prompt` | The question is about people generally, not the author's next step. (does not ask for help) |

Cases 1, 4, and 8 check the hardest boundary. A personal experience alone
(cases 1 and 8) still can be a `discussion_prompt`. An unresolved personal
concern plus a request for help (case 4) is a `help_request`.
